How skill gap analysis works

Capacitix analyzes the Student's completed assessment responses competency by competency. The current level is derived from the actual assessment percentage, while the required level comes from the competency definition. Students below 60% are identified as skill gaps. Scores below 40% are high-priority gaps, scores from 40% to 59% are medium-priority gaps, and Students without a completed assessment remain Not assessed.
