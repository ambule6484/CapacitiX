How Assessments Work

Your assessment result helps Capacitix understand your current competency and identify areas for improvement.

1
Required Competencies Defined
Your organization defines the competencies required for your role.
2
Assessment Generated by AI
AI generates questions from the required competencies for your role.
3
Trainer Assigns Assessment
Your trainer selects an available AI-generated assessment and assigns it to you.
4
Complete Assessment
Answer the competency-based questions assigned to you.
5
Automatic Evaluation
Your answers are checked against the stored correct answers and your score is calculated.
6
Skill Gap Analysis
Capacitix analyzes your competency-wise performance and identifies areas where additional learning is required.
