Understanding the Skill Heatmap

Scores come from Student assessments, competency mapping and practical evaluations. Strong is 80 or higher, Good is 60 to 79, Moderate is 40 to 59 and Critical is below 40. Grey cells have not been assessed yet and are left out of averages. The statistics above count individual skill scores, not Students.
